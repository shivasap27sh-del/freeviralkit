# 📋 Blog Post Essentials Checklist
### SEO + Ads + Performance + Monetization
> **All 6 sections are essential before publishing any blog post on FreeViralKit.**

---

## 1. SEO Basics

| # | Item | Requirement | Status |
|---|------|-------------|--------|
| 1.1 | **Title tag** | 50–60 chars, primary keyword first | `[ ]` |
| 1.2 | **Meta description** | 150–160 chars, CTA included | `[ ]` |
| 1.3 | **Focus keyword** | 1 primary + 3–5 related LSI keywords in tags | `[ ]` |
| 1.4 | **SEO-friendly URL** | Short, keyword-based slug (e.g. `youtube-shorts-viral-secrets`) | `[ ]` |
| 1.5 | **Header tags (H1–H3)** | One H1 (`title`), keywords in H2/H3 headings | `[ ]` |
| 1.6 | **Internal links** | 3–5 links to related blog posts + tool pages | `[ ]` |

> [!TIP]
> Place the primary keyword in the **first 3 words** of the title and the **first sentence** of the content for maximum SEO weight.

---

## 2. Content Quality

| # | Item | Requirement | Status |
|---|------|-------------|--------|
| 2.1 | **Word count** | 1,000–2,500 words minimum | `[ ]` |
| 2.2 | **E-E-A-T signals** | Author expertise shown — personal stories, data, experience | `[ ]` |
| 2.3 | **Original content** | No duplicate paragraphs, unique value proposition | `[ ]` |
| 2.4 | **Readability** | Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences), simple words, conversational tone | `[ ]` |
| 2.5 | **Post structure** | Intro → Body → FAQ → CTA (conclusion) | `[ ]` |
| 2.6 | **External links** | Cite 2–3 authority sources (YouTube Help, Google docs, studies) | `[ ]` |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Emotional storytelling** is mandatory. Every post should open with a relatable story, pain point, or personal anecdote — not a generic definition.

---

## 3. Images and Media

| # | Item | Requirement | Status |
|---|------|-------------|--------|
| 3.1 | **Image alt text** | Keyword-rich descriptive alt text on every image | `[ ]` |
| 3.2 | **Compressed images** | Under 100KB, WebP format preferred | `[ ]` |
| 3.3 | **Video / Infographic** | Embed a video or add an infographic to boost time-on-page | `[ ]` |

> [!NOTE]
> Currently using Unsplash URLs directly. For future posts, consider self-hosting compressed WebP images for better Core Web Vitals scores.

---

## 4. Technical SEO

| # | Item | Requirement | Status |
|---|------|-------------|--------|
| 4.1 | **Schema markup** | `Article` or `BlogPosting` JSON-LD (auto-generated by `[slug]/page.tsx`) | `[ ]` |
| 4.2 | **Page speed** | Load under 3 seconds (no `force-dynamic`, static prerender) | `[ ]` |
| 4.3 | **Mobile-friendly** | Responsive design, readable on all screen sizes | `[ ]` |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Never** use `export const dynamic = 'force-dynamic'` on blog pages. All blog posts must be statically prerendered for optimal crawl speed.

---

## 5. Ads Readiness (Google AdSense / Display)

| # | Item | Requirement | Status |
|---|------|-------------|--------|
| 5.1 | **Policy-safe content** | No prohibited topics (adult, gambling, violence, drugs) | `[ ]` |
| 5.2 | **Sufficient text** | Enough content for ads to appear — min 1,000 words, 4+ sections | `[ ]` |
| 5.3 | **Ad placement zones** | Header, sidebar, and in-content ad slots (via `InContentAd` component) | `[ ]` |

> [!TIP]
> The `InContentAd` component auto-injects ads every 4 sections. Ensure posts have **at least 5 H2 sections** so that 1–2 ad slots naturally appear within the content.

---

## 6. Engagement and Social Sharing

| # | Item | Requirement | Status |
|---|------|-------------|--------|
| 6.1 | **Open Graph tags** | `og:title`, `og:image`, `og:description` set via page metadata | `[ ]` |
| 6.2 | **Share buttons** | Facebook, X, WhatsApp, etc. (if component available) | `[ ]` |
| 6.3 | **Call to action** | End with a clear CTA: subscribe / comment / share / use a tool | `[ ]` |

> [!TIP]
> Every post should end with a CTA linking to **at least 2 FreeViralKit tools** relevant to the post topic.

---

## Quick Reference: Post Structure Template

```
## [Emotional Story / Personal Anecdote Hook]
   (200-300 words — relatable, builds empathy)

---

## [Core Topic Section 1 — H2 with keyword]
   (300-400 words — actionable, detailed)

## [Core Topic Section 2 — H2 with keyword]
   (300-400 words — examples, data)

## [Core Topic Section 3 — H2 with keyword]
   (300-400 words — advanced tips)

---

## Frequently Asked Questions
   (5 unique questions — NOT duplicated from other posts)
   (Each answer: 50-100 words, keyword-rich)

---

## [CTA Section — Tool Links + Emotional Close]
   (100-150 words — link to 2+ FreeViralKit tools)
```
